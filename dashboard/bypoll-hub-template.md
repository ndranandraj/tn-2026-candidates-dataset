Tamil Nadu has held **2 Assembly by-elections** since the May 2026 general election, both on October 6. TVK won both. Five more seats are vacant, with no dates announced. This page keeps every bypoll result in one place, next to the same seat's result in May 2026 and in 2021.

## October 2026: Madurantakam and Dharapuram

Both seats voted AIADMK in May. On May 25 both MLAs, K. Maragatham Kumaravel and P. Sathyabama, resigned and joined TVK, which fielded them again in the same seats. Both won. The AIADMK finished third in both. The full story is in [the analysis post](/posts/tn-bypolls-2026-madurantakam-dharapuram/).
